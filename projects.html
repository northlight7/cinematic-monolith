How a study
becomes a building.

The drawing and the model are the same object at different densities. Nothing is decided twice.

Draw

Plans, section and one perspective. If it does not work in section, it does not work.

Model

A dimensional model, built to the same dimensions as the drawing.

Walk

The camera enters the courtyard. Scale is checked from eye height.

Detail

Materials and junctions are resolved at 1:5 before anything is specified.
