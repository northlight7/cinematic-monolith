Three
beliefs.

They are not slogans. Each one changes a drawing.

01

Light is a material

A room is not finished until you know where the sun lands in it at four in the afternoon.

02

The section decides

If a plan works but the section does not, the building will not work.

03

Leave something out

The best rooms usually have one thing fewer than you expected.

How we
work.

Small projects, drawn carefully, with the client in the room while the decisions are still open.

Listen

A site visit and a long conversation before any drawing exists.

Test

Two or three sketch schemes, each answering a different question.

Resolve

One scheme carried through to 1:5 details.

Stay

On site through construction, not just at handover.
