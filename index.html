Study 001 / Field notes

The open heart.

The courtyard is a void through both floors. Light reaches the centre, while glass and timber mediate the rooms around it. A tree anchors the view from every level.

This is an unbuilt architectural concept. The live model includes separable floor assemblies, a daylight control, water, terrain and an interior camera journey.
